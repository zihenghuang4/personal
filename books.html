Books

Recent reads that have shaped my thinking on business, technology, and life.

Shoe Dog

Phil Knight

The memoir of Nike's founder, chronicling the company's early days from selling shoes out of his car to building a global brand.

Key Takeaways
  • Success rarely follows a straight path—embrace the chaos and uncertainty of building something new
  • Your team and the people you surround yourself with matter more than capital or strategy
  • Persistence through near-bankruptcy, supplier issues, and countless setbacks is what separates those who make it

The Experimentation Machine

Jess Bussgang

A framework for building successful ventures by treating startups as ongoing experiments, combining the rigor of scientific method with entrepreneurial creativity.

Key Takeaways
  • Treat your startup as a series of testable hypotheses—focus on learning what works rather than proving you're right
  • The best entrepreneurs rapidly cycle through build-measure-learn loops to validate assumptions before scaling
  • Combine vision with flexibility; be stubborn on vision but flexible on details as data reveals what customers actually want

Dopamine Nation

Anna Lembke

Finding balance in an age of indulgence, exploring how our brains respond to pleasure and the consequences of overstimulation.

Key Takeaways
  • Our brains are wired for scarcity, but we live in abundance—this mismatch drives addictive behaviors
  • The pleasure-pain balance is real; overindulgence in dopamine hits leads to a deficit that requires more to feel normal
  • Strategic self-restraint and intentional discomfort can reset your baseline and restore genuine pleasure

Deep Work

Cal Newport

Rules for focused success in a distracted world, arguing that the ability to concentrate without distraction is increasingly valuable.

Key Takeaways
  • Deep work is becoming rare and therefore more valuable—those who master it will thrive
  • Attention is finite; treat it like a resource and schedule blocks of uninterrupted focus
  • Shallow work feels productive but rarely moves the needle; ruthlessly minimize it to protect deep work time
