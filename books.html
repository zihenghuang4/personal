Books

Recent reads that have shaped my thinking on business, technology, and life.

Sapiens

Yuval Noah Harari · In Progress

A sweeping history of humankind, tracing our evolution from ancient ancestors to the present day, exploring how biology, culture, and technology have shaped our species and the world we live in.

Just Add Water

Katie Ledecky · In Progress

The memoir of Olympic swimmer Katie Ledecky, chronicling her journey from a young prodigy to a dominant force in the pool, sharing insights into her training, mindset, and the pursuit of excellence.

Deep Work

Cal Newport · Finished February 2026

Rules for focused success in a distracted world, arguing that the ability to concentrate without distraction is increasingly valuable.

Key Takeaways
  • Use time blocks to schedule time for deep and shallow work, and keep a scoreboard
  • Do not switch tasks easily - your brain lingers on the last one
  • Discard social media if it does not add tremendous values to your goals

Dopamine Nation

Anna Lembke · Finished January 2026

Finding balance in an age of indulgence, exploring how our brains respond to pleasure and the consequences of overstimulation.

Key Takeaways
  • The brain works to balance pleasure and pain; overindulgence reduces your baseline level of pleasure
  • Abstinence (4 weeks+) + self-binding
  • Deliberately introducing small doses of pain/discomfort helps resetting baseline

Shoe Dog

Phil Knight · Finished January 2026

The memoir of Nike's founder, chronicling the company's early days from selling shoes out of his car to building a global brand.

Key Takeaways
  • You don't have to create something entirely new to be considered a successful business.
  • Entrepreneurship = a LONG period of persistence, uncertainty, and endless problems.
  • Phil Knight didn't chase money, he chased excellence and aimed to create better products than everybody else.
  • Your team and the people you surround yourself with matter more than capital or strategy

The Experimentation Machine

Jeff Bussgang · Finished April 2025

A framework for building successful ventures by treating startups as ongoing experiments, combining the rigor of scientific method with entrepreneurial creativity.

Key Takeaways
  • Run every part of a startup like an experiment—test assumptions, measure outcomes, and iterate quickly (build an experimentation culture where testing is systematic, not ad-hoc—make it a core capability)
  • Use generative AI as your mini co-founder and experiment catalyst who specializes in information gathering and extraction
  • Build an AI-native/forward cutlure

How the World Works

Noam Chomsky · To Be Read

A collection of essays and interviews that explore the political, economic, and social forces shaping our world, offering critical insights into power, media, and global affairs.

Never Stop

Dan Hurley · To Be Read

The inspiring life and leadership lessons of a legendary coach who turned his University of Connecticut team into back-to-back NCAA champions—wisdom that goes far beyond the basketball court.

Outliers

Malcolm Gladwell · To Be Read

An exploration of the factors that contribute to high levels of success, arguing that we often overlook the role of opportunity, cultural background, and hard work in shaping extraordinary achievements.

Psychology of Money

Morgan Housel · To Be Read

Timeless lessons on wealth, greed, and happiness, exploring how our emotions and biases influence financial decisions and offering practical advice for building a healthy relationship with money.

The Histories

Herodotus · To Be Read

The Histories is Herodotus's account of the Greco-Persian Wars, offering insights into ancient Greek and Persian cultures, politics, and society.
