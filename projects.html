Calendar Tracker

Productivity · Time Tracking · 2026

I schedule every minute of my waking day using time blocks on Notion Calendar, synced with Google Calendar. This tool integrates with that calendar, breaks down how I spend my time across five categories, and tracks my deep work hours — inspired by Cal Newport's Deep Work. The goal is to gradually push deep work toward an optimal daily level.

What it does
  • Connects to Google Calendar and reads time block events by color
  • Categorizes time into five buckets: personal development, work, personal projects, chores & meals, and wasted time
  • Surfaces total deep work hours (work + personal projects) per day and week
  • Tracks trends over time to help nudge deep work toward a personal target
